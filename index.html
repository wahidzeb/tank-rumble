About Tank Rumble

Tank Rumble is a cartoon battle on a toy-room floor. You drive a little green toy tank around a play mat while it fires by itself, popping robot toys into bolts and coins.

Each chapter has 5 stages, with a boss fight on stage 5, across four chapters. After each stage you pick one of 12 powers, and the Garage has upgrades to buy.

3 quick tips

  1. Keep rolling in smooth circles. Stopping lets the robots' shots catch up with you.
  2. Pick up the coins and power-ups that robots drop.
  3. Put your first coins into Armour in the Garage. More health means you finish more stages and earn more coins.

How to play

  • 👆 Drag anywhere to drive your tank.
  • 💥 It fires by itself at the nearest robot.
  • 🎯 Want to aim? Use a second finger on the other side, or switch Aim to Manual.
  • ⭐ Grab drops: shields, rapid fire, triple shot, hearts and magnets.
  • 🏆 Clear 5 stages and beat the boss bot to finish a chapter.
  • 🔧 Spend coins in the Garage on upgrades and new tanks.
